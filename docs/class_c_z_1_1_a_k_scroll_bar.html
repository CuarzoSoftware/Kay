Detailed Description

Auto-hiding overlay scroll bar.

Used in pairs by AKScroll: a rail (the node itself, a three-patch image from the theme) with a draggable rounded handle. The bar appears while the position or size changes and fades out after one second of inactivity; hovering fades it back in and widens the handle, and dragging (or clicking the rail) drives the attached AKScroll's offset. A sizePercent() of 1 (content fully visible) keeps it hidden.
